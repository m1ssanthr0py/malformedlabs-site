About

Malformed Labs grew out of D3ada55's (Ashley Sequeira's) investigation of SuperBOX streaming boxes: cheap Android TV devices that turn home networks into infrastructure for other people's crime. Her findings, from Tencent beaconing and LAN poisoning to unauthenticated root access, were reported by Krebs on Security and told in full on Darknet Diaries episode 172, "SuperBox".

That work helped change how consumer IoT devices are viewed from a security perspective: not as gadgets, but as part of the threat landscape.

Research areas

What we hunt, and how.

  • Consumer IoT & supply chain

    Streaming boxes and other devices that ship compromised or open: the SuperBOX and BADBOX 2.0 families and their look-alikes.

  • Residential proxy & botnet infrastructure

    How compromised home connections are rented out, and how to tell the exit from the operator.

  • Internet-scan threat hunting

    Finding C2 panels and attacker infrastructure by what hosts run and how they change, not just by IP reputation.

  • Detection engineering

    Measuring what enrichment actually adds to SOC decisions, with honest false-positive numbers.
