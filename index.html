About

Malformed Labs grew out of research into SuperBOX streaming boxes, first published in 2025: cheap Android TV devices that turn home networks into infrastructure for other people's crime. The findings, from Tencent beaconing and LAN poisoning to unauthenticated root access, were reported by Krebs on Security and told in full on Darknet Diaries episode 172, "SuperBox".

That work helped change how consumer IoT devices are viewed from a security perspective: not as gadgets, but as part of the threat landscape. Today D3ada55, a researcher at Malformed Labs, carries it forward and is available to partners as a direct resource.

Research areas

What we hunt, and how.

  • Residential proxies

    How compromised home connections, from streaming boxes to other consumer devices, are rented out as proxy exits, and how to tell the exit from the operator behind it.

  • Weird outliers

    The hosts and devices that don't fit the pattern: odd services, strange certificates, traffic that shouldn't be there. Outliers are where campaigns like SuperBOX first show up.

  • Threats you may not consider

    The box under the TV, the gadget on the shelf, the supply chain behind them. Everyday devices that quietly become part of someone else's operation.
